# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Opening an Account

## What documents do I need to open a checking account?
You'll need a valid government-issued photo ID, your Social Security number or ITIN, and a minimum opening deposit of $50. Applications can be completed online in about 10 minutes or in person at any branch.

## Is there a minimum age to open an account on my own?
You must be 18 years or older to open an account independently. Custodial accounts for anyone younger require a parent or legal guardian listed as a joint owner on the account.

## How soon can I access my account after opening it?
Online applications are typically approved within minutes, and you can access your account through online banking and the mobile app immediately after approval. A physical debit card arrives by mail within 5-7 business days.
