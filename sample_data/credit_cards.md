# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Credit Cards

## What is the rewards rate on the Cashback Rewards card?
The Cashback Rewards card earns 3% cash back on groceries and dining, 2% on gas, and 1% on all other purchases, with no annual cap on rewards earned.

## Are there foreign transaction fees?
The Cashback Rewards card charges no foreign transaction fees. The Standard credit card charges a 3% foreign transaction fee on purchases made outside the country.

## Can I request a credit limit increase, and how?
Credit limit increase requests can be submitted through the mobile app under Card Settings, or by calling customer support. Requests are typically reviewed within 2-3 business days and may involve a credit check.
