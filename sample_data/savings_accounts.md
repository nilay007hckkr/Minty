# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Savings Accounts

## What is the current APY on a standard savings account?
The standard savings account earns 2.10% APY on all balances, with interest compounded daily and credited monthly. There is no minimum balance required to start earning interest.

## Is there a High-Yield Savings option?
Yes. The High-Yield Savings account earns 4.75% APY on balances up to $50,000, and 3.00% APY on any amount above that threshold. A minimum opening deposit of $100 is required, and the rate is variable.

## How many withdrawals can I make per month without a fee?
Savings accounts allow up to 6 fee-free withdrawals or transfers per statement cycle. Each additional withdrawal beyond the sixth incurs a $5 excessive-withdrawal fee.
