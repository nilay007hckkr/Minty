# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Retail Banking

## What is the daily ATM withdrawal limit?
The standard daily ATM withdrawal limit for most checking accounts is $1,000. However, Premium and Wealth Management account holders have a daily limit of $2,500. You can temporarily increase your daily ATM withdrawal limit for up to 24 hours by logging into your mobile app and navigating to the "Card Management" section.

## Are there any fees for using an out-of-network ATM?
Yes. If you use an ATM outside of our approved network, we charge a $3.00 fee per transaction. Additionally, the operator of the out-of-network ATM may also charge you a separate surcharge. Customers with a Premium checking account are eligible for reimbursement of up to 5 out-of-network ATM fees per statement cycle.

## How can I avoid the monthly maintenance fee on my checking account?
The standard checking account carries a $12 monthly maintenance fee. You can have this fee waived if you meet at least one of the following criteria during the statement cycle:
* Maintain a minimum daily balance of $1,500 or more.
* Set up and receive direct deposits totaling $500 or more per month.
* Link the checking account to an active residential mortgage with us.

## What is the cut-off time for sending a domestic wire transfer?
To ensure same-day processing, domestic wire transfers must be submitted and approved by 4:00 PM Eastern Time on a regular business day. Wire transfers submitted after 4:00 PM, or on weekends and federal holidays, will be processed on the next business day. The fee for an outgoing domestic wire transfer is $25.
